About Us

A fresh, secure stay in the heart of Waterfalls

Warthog Guest Lodge offers short and long stay accommodation in a quiet, secure residential setting in Parktown, Waterfalls — close enough to the city and airport for convenience, far enough for a peaceful night's rest.

Every room comes with clean en-suite bathrooms, hot water, and the comforts of home — backed by solar power so you're never left without light or WiFi.
